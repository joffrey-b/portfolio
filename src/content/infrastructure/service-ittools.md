IT-Tools bundles dozens of everyday developer and sysadmin utilities, base64 and JWT decoders, token and password generators, unit converters, regex testers, into one self-hosted app, instead of relying on random public websites for the same tasks.

Deployed through the generic Compose stack alongside the other lightweight utility containers.
